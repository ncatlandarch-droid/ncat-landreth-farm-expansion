National Uniqueness & Strategic Significance: The Nation's Sole Undergraduate HBCU Pipeline
North Carolina Agricultural and Technical State University (NC A&T) houses the only undergraduate program of Landscape Architecture (Bachelor of Science in Landscape Architecture [BSLA]) accredited by the Landscape Architectural Accreditation Board (LAAB) at any Historically Black College or University (HBCU) in the nation. Every single undergraduate student seeking an accredited HBCU degree pathway into the profession must come through North Carolina A&T. This capacity building initiative establishes an integrated computational design, Internet of Things (IoT) environmental sensing, and digital twin curriculum—ensuring the nation's foundational diversity pipeline leads the discipline in technological excellence, professional certifications, and community-engaged practice.

1 The Problem, Institutional Need & Strategic Opportunity Context & Urgency
The Modern Landscape Industry Gap

The landscape architecture and civil planning professions are undergoing the most rapid technological realignment in 50 years. Top-tier design firms, regional planning agencies, and conservation organizations now demand graduates skilled in:

  • Artificial Intelligence (AI)-augmented spatial intelligence & site analysis
  • Environmental Internet of Things (IoT) sensor integration (soil hydrology, microclimate thermal)
  • Building Information Modeling (BIM) coordination (Vectorworks Landmark)
  • High-resolution unmanned aerial vehicle (drone) photogrammetry & 3D digital twins

Without institutional investment, historically underrepresented students remain locked out of high-wage, high-impact computational design positions.

NC A&T's Unique Position & Momentum

Rather than starting from scratch, North Carolina A&T faculty have already built substantial digital capacity through Evans-Allen research and departmental innovation:

  • AVA V.4: Adaptive Visualization Assistant (AVA V.4) — Academic AI site intelligence & Sustainable SITES Initiative (SITES v2) 250-point ecological rating engine (institutional visualization asset)
  • Modular Digital Curricula: Standardized Canvas Learning Management System (LMS) course cartridges (.imscc) and digital rubrics
  • Open Regional GIS Data Services: Live county parcel fabric, United States Geological Survey (USGS) contours, and United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS) Soil Survey Geographic Database (SSURGO) soils Application Programming Interfaces (APIs)
  • 3 Completed Drone Surveys: High-resolution 3D photogrammetry models of campus core and research farm living laboratories

The Gap: While AVA provides cutting-edge spatial and ecological visualization, this capability must be formally integrated into accredited coursework and validated through peer-reviewed research.

SFRIC Multi-Site Living Laboratories & The Community Design Center (CDC)

The Small Farm Research and Innovation Center (SFRIC) encompasses a diverse multi-site living laboratory network—including the 492-acre research farm, Inspiration Courtyard, Holland Bowl, and regional agricultural partners. To scale community impact, SFRIC is actively planning a dedicated Community Design Center (CDC) and is currently building strategic partnerships with the City of Greensboro, municipal planning and parks departments, local community leaders, and neighborhood grassroots organizations. Our Bachelor of Science in Landscape Architecture (BSLA) program connects directly to this developing center as its core design, visualization, and spatial modeling engine.

3 The 4-Year Scaffolded Curricular Framework The Pedagogical Core

A core strength of the proposal is its intentional scaffolding. Instead of abruptly replacing hand drafting with automated AI, the curriculum follows a rigorous progression: Design + Observe → Measure → Model → Lead. Hand drawing trains the eye and hand; digital tools expand analytical and spatial power.

Year 1: Freshman
OBSERVE & REPRESENT
Hand drafting, lineweight, 15 weekly field sketches → Regional Geographic Information Systems (GIS) data as observation lens → Module 14 digital bridge → Vectorworks Landmark Building Information Modeling (BIM) spring start.
Year 2: Sophomore
MEASURE & INTERPRET
Real-world empirical data collection at SFRIC farm: Soil Survey Geographic Database (SSURGO) soil sensors, Forward-Looking Infrared (FLIR) microclimate thermography, drone aerial surveys, and flood zone mapping.
Year 3: Junior
MODEL & TEST
AVA Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) site analysis, Sustainable SITES Initiative (SITES v2) 250-point ecological rating, stormwater modeling, USDA Environmental Quality Incentives Program (EQIP) conservation cost estimating, scenario testing.
Year 4: Senior
SYNTHESIZE & LEAD
Senior Capstone digital twins, Boston Dynamics Spot quadruped robot/Light Detection and Ranging (LiDAR) surveying, participatory community charrettes, Landscape Architect Registration Examination (LARE) licensing exam prep & jury defense.

6 Specific Abilities Acquired Through the Process Competencies & ROI
1. Student Professional & Computational Abilities

Graduates will possess an unmatched competitive edge: fluent in hand diagramming, Vectorworks Landmark Building Information Modeling (BIM) drafting, Python-assisted Geographic Information Systems (GIS) spatial queries, field sensor deployment, and generative AI prompt engineering. They will understand how to design climate-resilient landscapes backed by quantifiable empirical data (Sustainable SITES Initiative [SITES v2] scores, runoff hydrographs, USDA Environmental Quality Incentives Program [EQIP] funding models).

2. Student Licensing & Career Velocity (LARE Prep)

By synthesizing site engineering, construction detailing, and environmental codes into comprehensive Capstone studio projects and professional portfolio defense, students will enter the workforce with robust preparation for the Landscape Architect Registration Examination (LARE), shortening the path to licensed professional practice.

3. Faculty Scholarship & Tenured Research Capacity

All 4 program faculty gain direct, funded research and pedagogy credentials. Tenure-track faculty build rigorous grant management experience and high-impact publications presenting at the Council of Educators in Landscape Architecture (CELA) and the Environmental Design Research Association (EDRA), positioning NC A&T to successfully compete for larger National Science Foundation (NSF), USDA Agriculture and Food Research Initiative (AFRI), and private foundation grants.

4. Institutional Stature & 1890 National Leadership

North Carolina A&T becomes recognized by the American Society of Landscape Architects (ASLA), the Council of Educators in Landscape Architecture (CELA), the Environmental Design Research Association (EDRA), and the USDA National Institute of Food and Agriculture (NIFA) as the premier national incubator for diversity and technological excellence in the built environment, driving student recruitment and donor engagement.
