1. MULTIPLE SECTIONS NEEDED: LDAR 147 (Design Foundations) has only 1 seat left in a room that holds 16. If even 2–3 more students deposit, a second section of LDAR 147 is mandatory. Dr. Yang would need to teach two sections — requiring a second room and adjusted schedule. LDAR 103 (Discovery) also only has 7 seats remaining.

At 15+ freshmen, we need additional laptops and license seats immediately. Lead time for university procurement is typically 4–6 weeks — which means orders must go in NOW.
