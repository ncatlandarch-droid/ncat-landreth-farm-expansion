Strategic Positioning & Operational Transition — Fall 2026 through Spring 2028
NC A&T State University · CAES · NRED Department Prepared by W. Chris Harrison July 13, 2026

1. Executive Summary

This document outlines the 2-year faculty staffing transition for the Landscape Architecture (LDAR) program at North Carolina A&T State University. The plan supports the strategic pivot from a "Generalist" model to the "NCAT LandArch Way" — a High-Tech, High-Touch specialist model designed to secure LAAB accreditation and distinguish NC A&T from regional competitors.

Operational Phasing

Phase 1 — Transition (2026–2027): Utilizes three adjuncts (Christin, Anna, Preston) to manage "Gap" courses created by curriculum restructuring.

Phase 2 — Steady State (2027–2028): Consolidates to two adjuncts (Christin, Preston) to optimize budget while maintaining the 5.0 FTE required for LAAB accreditation.

5. Strategic Gap Management (LDAR 400)

To manage the curriculum transition without triggering Banner "Repeat Course" errors, the plan utilizes LDAR 400 (Special Problems) as an administrative bridge course with distinct sections:
