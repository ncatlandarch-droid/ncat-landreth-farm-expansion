Strategic Acquisition Insights

Institutional planning considerations for NC A&T State University leadership

📍 Adjacency to NC A&T Farm

Parcel 118733 (3401 McConnell Rd) is located just 0.4 miles east of the NC A&T University Farm entrance (3110 E McConnell Rd).

Acquiring this assemblage creates a massive research corridor connecting McConnell Road north to Ward Road and Wilsonwood Road, enabling large-scale field research expansion without crossing major arterial highways.

Opportunity: Creates a continuous research belt suitable for agronomy, robotics, livestock grazing, and environmental monitoring.

⚖️ PUV Rollback Tax Implications

The properties carry $3,767,600 in deferred agricultural value under North Carolina's Present-Use Value (PUV) statute (G.S. 105-277.2).

When private buyers take land out of agricultural production, Guilford County triggers a mandatory 3-year plus current year tax rollback with interest.

Acquisition Note: As a constitutional agency of the State of North Carolina, property acquired by NC A&T / The State of NC becomes exempt from ad valorem property taxes under NC Constitution Art. V, Sec. 2(3).

🛰️ SFRIC & Digital Twin Synergy

This 492-acre contiguous block aligns directly with the Small Farm Research and Innovation Center (SFRIC) Digital Twin roadmap.

The continuous airspace and terrain profile offer an ideal proving ground for autonomous tractors, robotic field sensors, and UAS multi-spectral drone mapping.

Tech Capability: Ready for RTK-GPS base stations and IoT LoRaWAN mesh networks connecting back to the main campus and farm facilities.
