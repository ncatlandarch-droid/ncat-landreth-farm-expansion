3. Five Essential Talking Points Core Script
Point 1 Zero Syllabus or Catalog Burden (No Mandated Changes)
"I want to clear up a major misconception: this grant does not alter our approved course catalog, syllabi, or LAAB accreditation in any way. It is strictly an optional experiential enrichment resource—providing extracurricular student workshops, software access, and drone/sensor tools that faculty can draw from only if they choose. If an instructor doesn't want to touch it, they don't have to."
• Eliminates Dallas's fear that unapproved catalog changes or accreditation risks are being imposed.
Point 2 Dallas is 100% Off the Hook (Zero Labor for Her)
"Dallas has a full plate with coordinator duties. Dallas, I want to make sure you know: you have zero obligation on this grant. You do not need to serve as Co-PI, do any reporting, or manage technology. That initial placeholder was strictly a courtesy for future summer support if you wanted it. Our multi-college team handles 100% of the administration."
• Dallas opposed this because she feared extra work. Removing the duty evaporates her motivation to resist.
Point 3 The Grant Directly Funds the Technical Labor (2 GRAs + 2 UGAs)
"Dallas raised a fair question about technical maintenance and faculty expertise. But that's exactly why the budget is structured to fund 2 Graduate Research Assistants ($16k/yr stipends) and 2 Paid Undergraduate Assistants ($4k/yr). These funded students manage the hardware, troubleshoot software, and run student tutorials. It brings over $120,000 directly into student financial support for NRED."
• Chairs love funded Graduate Assistant lines. It solves the technical labor problem without faculty effort.
Point 4 The $750,000 Integrated Project Upgrade & Powerhouse Coalition
